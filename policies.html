Customer information

Our policies.

01

Shipping policy

Orders are processed after payment confirmation. Processing times may vary depending on product availability, order size, and customer verification requirements. Primendowned currently delivers across Nigeria.

Same-day or next-day delivery may be available for eligible Lagos orders depending on location and product availability. Outside Lagos, deliveries through trusted logistics partners typically take 2–7 business days. Shipping costs depend on delivery location, package size, urgency, and provider rates. Tracking may be provided where applicable.

Payment on delivery is available only in selected Lagos areas and may not apply to all products. Customers are encouraged to inspect products on delivery before acceptance. Primendowned is not responsible for delays caused by logistics providers, weather, security issues, public holidays, or other circumstances beyond reasonable control. Customers are responsible for accurate delivery details; Primendowned is not liable for failed deliveries caused by incorrect addresses or contact details.

02

Refund policy

Refunds may be considered if the wrong product was delivered, a product significantly differs from the agreed specification, it arrives damaged due to verified delivery issues, or an order cannot be fulfilled after confirmed payment.

Refunds do not apply to change of mind, minor cosmetic wear disclosed before sale, third-party software issues, or damage caused by misuse, accidents, liquid exposure, or unauthorized repairs. Report issues within 24–48 hours of receiving the product. All requests are reviewed before approval. Approved refunds may take 3–10 business days depending on payment providers and banking systems.

03

Warranty policy

Warranty coverage varies by product category and condition. Products may include a testing warranty, limited hardware warranty, or manufacturer warranty where applicable. Warranty details are communicated before purchase.

Coverage does not include physical or liquid damage, electrical surge damage, misuse, third-party software corruption, or unauthorized repairs or modifications. Claims require proof of purchase, an invoice or receipt, and product details. Device inspection may be required. Replacement or repair decisions are made by Primendowned based on technical evaluation.

Privacy policy

Primendowned may collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, delivery addresses, payment-related information, and device inquiry information. We use customer information to process orders, provide support, improve customer experience and business operations, send order updates, and respond to inquiries.

Customer information is handled securely and is not sold to third parties. Trusted providers may process information for payments, analytics, or logistics. Customers may receive promotional communication unless they opt out. The website may use cookies and analytics tools to improve functionality and user experience. This policy may be updated as business operations evolve.
